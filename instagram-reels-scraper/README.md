# Instagram Reels Scraper - Plays & Comments, $1.5/1K

Free sample output (32 rows, `sample.csv`) from the Apify actor **instagram-reels-scraper**.

Actor page: https://apify.com/transparent_meteorite/instagram-reels-scraper

**Disclosure: I maintain this actor.** This repo is a free sample of its real output so you can judge the columns before running it. Nothing here is a review or endorsement by a third party.

## What it does
Scrape Instagram reels from any public account without login: play counts, likes, comments, duration, caption, hashtags, music or original audio, video and audio URLs, tagged users, collaborators, pinned flag and the latest comments. Reel URLs work too.

## Columns in sample.csv
`type`, `shortCode`, `url`, `caption`, `hashtags`, `likesCount`, `commentsCount`, `videoViewCount`, `videoPlayCount`, `videoDuration`, `timestamp`

## Data source and privacy
Public web pages and public ad/job/product listings. Personal data is removed or masked (emails look like `a***@domain`). The sample was generated on 2026-10-12 and is a snapshot, not a maintained dataset. Use the actor for current data and check the source's terms for your use.
